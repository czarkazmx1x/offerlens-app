Scoring settings

Return trip adds that % of the offer's miles (and drive time at your avg speed). $/mi is payout ÷ total miles. $/hr is after mileage cost. GOOD needs both GOOD thresholds; falling below either PASS threshold is PASS; anything in between is CAUTION. Apartment drops, heavy items, ID checks and (for non-shopping offers) item count add time, which lowers $/hr. The app's own time estimate already includes shopping. The tip line shows what the offer scores if the customer removes the tip.
